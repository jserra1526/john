Component usage

A full bar means that stock is the limit on cost.

How it works: each batch is a weighted mix of components, so its fat and protein are the kg-weighted averages of what goes in. The page solves this as a linear programme (two-phase simplex) that minimises total component cost, subject to each batch hitting its kg, staying inside its fat and protein limits, and total use of each component staying within stock.

Check results against your local labelling rules. In the UK and EU, for example, protein standardisation of drinking milk may only use milk retentate, permeate or lactose.
