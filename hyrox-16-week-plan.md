# 16-Week HYROX Plan — Open Singles

**Built for:** an experienced hybrid athlete (has raced HYROX), training **6 days/week**, with full access to a **HYROX-style gym** (SkiErg, rower, sled, wall balls, sandbag, kettlebells).
**Goal:** a faster Open finish time. Race day = **Saturday of Week 16**.

---

## 1. Set your paces first (Week 1)

All running is prescribed off a **5k time trial** you run on Tuesday of Week 1.

| Zone | How to set it | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy / Z2** | 5k pace + 75–90 s/km | Conversational, nasal-breathing possible |
| **Tempo** | 5k pace + 25–35 s/km | Comfortably hard, ~1 hr race effort |
| **Threshold** | 5k pace + 12–20 s/km | Hard but controlled, 3–4 word answers |
| **Race pace (RP)** | Your target 1 km split in HYROX (usually 5k pace + 20–40 s/km) | What you'll hold between stations |
| **VO2** | 5k pace or slightly faster | Very hard, short reps only |

**Week 1 station benchmarks** (Saturday, fresh, full rest between): 1000 m SkiErg · 1000 m Row · 100 wall balls for time · 50 m sled push + 50 m sled pull at race weight (time each). Re-test in Week 9 (inside the full sim) and Week 13.

### Open race weights (for reference)

| Station | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Sled push (incl. sled) | 152 kg | 102 kg |
| Sled pull (incl. sled) | 103 kg | 78 kg |
| Farmers carry | 2 × 24 kg | 2 × 16 kg |
| Sandbag lunges | 20 kg | 10 kg |
| Wall balls | 6 kg to 3.0 m | 4 kg to 2.7 m |

## 2. The weekly structure

| Day | Session | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Strength A (lower) + compromised finisher | Leg strength for sleds/lunges, running on tired legs |
| **Tue** | Run quality (intervals) | Threshold & race-pace speed |
| **Wed** | Aerobic engine (Z2 run + erg) | Volume without fatigue |
| **Thu** | Strength B (pull / upper / carries) + station work | Grip, pulling, wall-ball & ski capacity |
| **Fri** | Long easy run | Aerobic base, durability |
| **Sat** | **Key HYROX session** (sim / compromised running) | Race specificity — the most important day |
| **Sun** | Rest | Full rest or a 20–30 min walk / mobility |

**Weeks 4, 8 and 12 are deloads** (~40% less volume, same movement patterns). Don't skip them — this is where adaptation happens.

## 3. Phase 1 — Base (Weeks 1–4)

*Goal: build aerobic volume, re-groove station technique, lay a strength base.*

### Strength A (Mon)
- Back squat 4 × 6 @ ~70–75% 1RM
- Romanian deadlift 3 × 8
- Bulgarian split squat 3 × 10/leg
- Heavy sled push 4 × 20 m (≈ race weight +20–40 kg)
- **Finisher:** 3 rounds — 400 m run @ RP + 20 wall balls

### Strength B (Thu)
- Trap-bar deadlift 4 × 5
- Push press 4 × 6
- Pull-ups 4 × max (or weighted 4 × 6)
- Bent-over row 3 × 10
- Farmers carry 4 × 40 m (heavier than race weight)
- **Station work:** 5 × 250 m SkiErg hard, 1 min rest · 3 × 25 wall balls unbroken

### Wed (aerobic)
30–40 min Z2 run + 20 min alternating SkiErg/Row (5 min each, Z2).

| Week | Tue — Run quality | Fri — Long run | Sat — Key session |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **5k time trial** (sets your paces) | 10 km easy | **Station benchmarks** (see above) + 3 km easy |
| 2 | 6 × 1 km @ threshold, 90 s rest | 11 km easy | 4 × (1 km @ RP + 500 m Ski + 25 wall balls), 2 min rest |
| 3 | 4 × 2 km @ threshold, 2 min rest | 12 km easy | 4 × (1 km @ RP + 500 m Row + 50 m sled push + 50 m sled pull) |
| 4 *(deload)* | 4 × 1 km @ threshold | 8 km easy | **Half sim:** 8 × (1 km @ RP + half station*) at ~80% effort |

\*Half stations: 500 m Ski · 25 m push · 25 m pull · 40 m burpee broad jumps · 500 m Row · 100 m farmers · 50 m lunges · 50 wall balls.

## 4. Phase 2 — Build (Weeks 5–8)

*Goal: raise threshold, get stronger, start linking running and stations at race weights.*

### Strength A (Mon)
- Back squat 5 × 5 @ 75–80%
- RDL 4 × 6
- Walking lunges with sandbag 4 × 30 m (race weight +5–10 kg)
- **Finisher:** 4 rounds — 1 km run @ RP + 50 m heavy sled push

### Strength B (Thu)
- Trap-bar deadlift 5 × 4
- Push press 4 × 5
- Weighted pull-ups 4 × 5
- Single-arm row 3 × 10/arm
- Farmers carry 4 × 50 m heavy
- **Station work:** 4 × (500 m Ski @ race effort + 30 wall balls), 90 s rest

### Wed (aerobic)
45–55 min Z2 run + 15 min Z2 row + 6 × 20 s strides.

| Week | Tue — Run quality | Fri — Long run | Sat — Key session |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 5 × 1.5 km @ threshold, 90 s rest | 13 km easy | **Short-run sim:** 8 × (500 m run @ RP + full station) |
| 6 | 3 × 3 km @ threshold, 2 min rest | 14 km easy | **Back half:** 4 × 1 km @ RP + full stations 5–8 (Row 1000 · Farmers 200 m · Lunges 100 m · Wall balls 100) |
| 7 | 8 × 1 km @ RP −5 s/km, 60 s rest | 15 km easy | **Front half:** 4 × 1 km @ RP + full stations 1–4 (Ski 1000 · Push 50 m · Pull 50 m · BBJ 80 m) |
| 8 *(deload)* | 5 × 1 km @ threshold | 10 km easy | Station technique EMOM 30 min (rotate all 8, easy) + 5 km tempo |

## 5. Phase 3 — Race-specific (Weeks 9–12)

*Goal: hold race pace on compromised legs; make stations feel smaller than race day.*

### Strength A (Mon)
- Back squat 4 × 3 @ 82–85%
- Box jumps 4 × 5 (power)
- Sandbag lunges 4 × 50 m @ race weight, minimal rest
- **Finisher:** 3 rounds — 1 km @ RP + 50 m sled push + 50 m sled pull (race weight)

### Strength B (Thu)
- Deadlift 4 × 3 @ 80–85%
- Weighted pull-ups 4 × 4
- Push press 3 × 5
- **Station work:** 5 × 30 wall balls unbroken (60 s rest) · 3 × 200 m farmers at race weight, no drops

### Wed (aerobic)
50–60 min Z2 run *or* 40 min run + 20 min Ski/Row. Keep it truly easy.

| Week | Tue — Run quality | Fri — Long run | Sat — Key session |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 4 × 2 km @ threshold, 90 s rest | 16 km easy | **Full HYROX simulation** @ ~85–90% effort. Time every run and station — this is your mid-plan benchmark |
| 10 | 10 × 1 km @ RP, 45 s rest | 14 km, last 3 km @ RP | **Overload:** 8 × (1 km @ RP + half station at heavier load / +25% reps) |
| 11 | 2 × 4 km @ tempo, 3 min rest | 16 km easy | **Full sim — dress rehearsal #1** @ race effort. Race kit, race-day breakfast, pacing plan |
| 12 *(deload)* | 5 × 1 km @ RP | 10 km easy | 3 × (2 km @ RP + 2 stations), relaxed |

*Goal: sharpest fitness of the block. Volume holds, intensity is highest.*

- **Mon:** Squat 3 × 3 @ 85% · lunges 3 × 50 m · finisher 3 × (800 m @ RP + 30 wall balls)
- **Wed:** 45 min Z2 + strides
- **Thu:** Deadlift 3 × 3 @ 85% · pull-ups 3 × 5 · Ski 4 × 500 m @ race pace · farmers 2 × 200 m

| Week | Tue — Run quality | Fri — Long run | Sat — Key session |
|---|---|---|---|

| 14 | 8 × 1 km @ RP −5 s/km, 60 s rest | 12 km easy | 8 × (1 km @ RP + 50% station), crisp and fast — leave feeling good |

## 8. Race-day strategy

- **Runs:** start Run 1 at RP +5 s/km. The first 1 km almost always goes out too fast — the goal is even splits on runs 1–8.
- **Roxzone:** jog through it, don't walk. It's free time to win.
- **SkiErg / Row:** settle at a sustainable pace (~your benchmark 1000 m pace +4–6 s/500 m). Breathe, don't sprint.
- **Sleds:** short, choppy steps for the push; hand-over-hand rhythm and use the legs for the pull. Don't stop mid-lane if you can help it.
- **Burpee broad jumps:** steady rhythm beats fast bursts.
- **Farmers:** aim for zero drops.
- **Lunges:** small consistent steps, knees tap, keep moving.
- **Wall balls:** pre-plan sets (e.g. 25-25-20-15-15) and short, fixed rests — don't wait until failure.

## 9. Fuel, recovery & adjustments

- **Sleep:** 7.5–9 hrs; it's the biggest recovery lever on a 6-day week.
- **Protein:** ~1.6–2.2 g/kg bodyweight/day; carbs scale with training (higher on Tue/Sat).
- **Key sessions:** practise race-day fuelling in Weeks 11 and 13 (e.g. a gel ~20 min before the start and one around halfway if your race is >75 min).
- **Missed a session?** Prioritise in this order: **Sat > Tue > Mon > Thu > Fri > Wed**. Never cram two hard days together.
- **Feeling run down / niggles:** swap Wed or Fri runs for SkiErg/Row/bike at the same effort; drop the Monday finisher before dropping the main lifts.
- **Too easy?** Add 1–2 km to Fri and Wed, not more intervals. **Too hard?** Repeat the previous week before moving on.
