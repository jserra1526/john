Milestones

How this works. Each year, super grows at the chosen return and receives contributions less 15% tax. From retirement it moves to an account-based pension: earnings are untaxed and at least the legal minimum (4% under 65, 5% from 65, rising with age) is drawn. Spending is paid from super first, then shares. Any minimum drawdown you don't need is reinvested in shares. From 67 the Age Pension is estimated for a homeowning couple using the lower of the assets test (full below $481,500, $3 a fortnight less per $1,000 above) and the income test with deeming (0.75% / 2.75%). Thresholds are September 2025 values, indexed to inflation. $30k of cars and contents is counted as assets.
